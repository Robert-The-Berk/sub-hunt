    How to play
    • Active ping (amber): click a square. If the sub is within 10 nmi (5 squares), you may get a return showing its range as a ring. Big subs echo reliably; small ones often don't. Pinging also warns the sub, so it speeds up and dodges.
    • No return doesn't prove the sub isn't there. A small sub can sit inside the circle and still not echo.
    • Sonobuoys (teal) listen silently every turn up to 16 nmi away and report a bearing, not a range. Fast subs are loud; slow ones are hard to hear.
    • Depth charges (red) hit a 3×3 area where the sub is now, before it moves. Run out of charges or let it reach the escape line on the west edge and you lose.
    • Every action takes one turn. After each turn the sub moves.
